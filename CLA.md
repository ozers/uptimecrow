# Contributor Licence Agreement

Short version: you keep the copyright to what you write. You give the UptimeCrow
maintainer a licence broad enough to ship your contribution — in this AGPL-3.0
repository and, if it ever happens, in a commercially licensed edition of the
same software.

Why this exists: UptimeCrow is AGPL-3.0 and intends to stay that way, but the
[open-core boundary](./OPEN_CORE.md) means some editions may be licensed
differently. Without this agreement, merging a single outside patch would make
that impossible for everyone, forever — including for the contributor's own
code. Grafana, GitLab, and Mattermost use the same mechanism for the same
reason.

If you would rather not sign it, that is a completely reasonable position. Open
an issue describing the change instead; a well-written bug report or design
proposal is worth as much as a patch, and needs no paperwork.

## How to sign

Add this line to your pull request description, with your real name and the date:

```
I have read CLA.md and I accept its terms.
Signed: Ada Lovelace <ada@example.com>, 2026-09-07
```

That is the whole process — no separate account, no external service. Signing
once covers every contribution you make to this repository.
